Recruitment

Process

We recruit members to make sure our weekly internal meetups stay healthy.

To be recruited, a majority vote and no vetos of the internal recruiting council is needed.

We are at minimum looking for the following in new members, though other factors will also be considered in your application.

  • Willing to attend at least 1 of the regular weekly meetups.
  • Drive and interest to improve and help the team improve at Netrunner.
  • Intent to participate in the Netrunner competitive scene beyond the current competitive season.

The following represents whether we are actively looking for people in a particular timezone.

  • America Timezone (Thu 7pm E(S)T): Recruiting Open
  • EMEA Timezone (Interleaved Thu/Fri 7pm CE(S)T): Recruiting Closed
  • APAC Timezone (Sun 9pm SGT): Recruiting Open
